How it works

From sign-up to your first practice log in a couple of minutes.

Create an account

Use your email and a password. Confirm your email if asked.

Open your simulator

You get a private workspace. Try the built-in demo or start logging your own statuses.

Practice and review

Edit logs, check timers and violations, and print the days you want to discuss.

Please note. This is an educational simulator. It is not a certified ELD, does not replace a registered ELD, and its results are not a compliance determination. Hours-of-service rules have exceptions (for example adverse driving and short-haul) that this training model may not cover.
